# Arghanzza Bestiary

> Enemies specific to Arghanzza — the body-horror forest where the hive occupies
> the environment. For the region itself, see `../Regions/Arghanzza/`.

## Enemies

### Mud-Remembered Husks

Repeat or **anticipate** the player's routes/patterns. Tied to the hive-mud
mechanic (the mud records your weight/rhythm). See
`../Regions/Arghanzza/traversal.md`.

### Pine-Bound Bodies

Human bodies **fused into trunks** that detach when disturbed.

### Root Hands

Terrain hazards that **grab ankles.**

### Whisper Mimics

Invisible/hidden lures using **NPC or sword lines** (mimicked dialogue). Can use
recent NPC dialogue heard via hive-mud.

### Pyre Husks

Burned bodies that were **not properly severed.** Fire may **wake** them unless
prepared correctly — pyrecraft matters (see
`../Regions/Arghanzza/region-arghanzza.md`). This is the botched-fire case of the
general [kill-resolution rule](../Mechanics/kill-resolution.md): proper fire is
permanent; bad fire is just another mutation method.

### Thorn Choir

Multiple bodies connected by **thorns/root command** — a linked group.

### Ear Stones

Environmental **listening nodes.** Sound near them increases forest awareness and
can reset paths. (Also "silent rocks" that may absorb/dim the sword's glow.)

### Breathing Pine Sentinel (miniboss)

Large tree-body miniboss that **inhales mist and detects movement.** Tied to the
breathing-cycle traversal rule (move on exhale).

## Boss — Hearthroot / Hearthmother

Once a beloved baker / matriarch / communal hearth figure. Her hearth/oven became
the **infection center.** Now fused with roots and the old village hearth, still
"feeding" the village.

Fight could progress:

```text
village / cottage  →  oven-root body  →  soul-side memory of a warm kitchen
                                          decaying into hive
```

A clean expression of Arghanzza's theme: **the hive conquered through kindness,
habit, and recognition** (see `../Regions/Arghanzza/region-arghanzza.md`).

## Note on detection & spoofing

The sword's glow detects hidden pine-husks, hive-mud, active root nodes, and
environmental organs — but silent rocks dim it, and the hive may **spoof** the
glow under pressure here. (See `../Sword/glow-and-detection.md`.)
