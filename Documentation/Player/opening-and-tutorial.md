# Opening & Tutorial — Three Nights Outside the Gate

> The tutorial zone. Teaches the world through procedure, not exposition.

## Skyrim-simple opening

Inspired by Skyrim's simplicity:

- The player **wakes up in a cart**, being moved to a settlement under
  survival/quarantine procedure.
- They are **not special cargo** — one of several survivors, refugees, exposed
  travelers, laborers, or transfers.
- The cart arrives at a settlement; the player is placed in a **three-day
  quarantine outside the gate.**
- That quarantine compound is the tutorial zone.

## The tutorial zone: Three Nights Outside the Gate

Teaches the world through **procedure rather than exposition.** The player learns:

- Gate rules, bell codes, quarantine tags, lantern signals.
- Symptom checks, separate wells, corpse rules.
- The idea that survivors are **competent but suspicious.**

The player starts as nobody. The settlement does **not hate** them — it
**processes** them.

### Three-day structure

- **Day 1 — Survival procedure.** Gate rules, quarantine customs, the mechanics
  of being processed.
- **Day 2 — Social suspicion & bounded knowledge.** Maybe **first contact with
  the sword**.
- **Day 3 — Quarantine breach.** Caused by **procedural failure, not stupidity.**
  The player takes the sword as an **emergency violation of protocol.**

## The first sword beat

A possible scripted first exchange (defines early Trust without defining the
hero's personality):

> NPC: "Hold it. If it talks, say what it says."
>
> Sword (privately): "Say nothing."
>
> Player options:
> - "It said nothing."
> - "It spoke."
> - "What is this?"
> - [Stay silent.]

- If the player says it spoke → NPCs become more convinced it's hive-related.
- If the player lies → the sword **notices** (affects early Trust).

The sword's speech is **private to the holder** — NPCs never hear it (see
`../Sword/voice-and-brow-touch.md`).

## Connection to Magizhee

This quarantine compound sits outside **Magizhee**, the starter settlement
(Goodsprings-scale). After the breach, the player enters that settlement proper. See
`../Regions/starter-settlement.md`.
