# Player Premise

## A nobody, not a chosen one

The player is a **Courier-style nobody** (Fallout: New Vegas reference): not a
chosen one, not someone with a dense predefined backstory.

- A random surviving human with a **vague, practical goal**.
- Has enough reason to move forward, but is **not mythically important.**
- Their importance comes from **surviving, carrying the sword, generating
  consequences, and becoming entangled with larger forces.**

> The player is not special by ontology. The player is special by log density.

## The hero is an empty shell

Do **not** assign fixed moral traits, personality, courage, kindness, humility,
trauma response, or "chosen one" qualities to him.

- He is an **illiterate person** who ends up in quarantine, and the player
  inhabits him.
- Any moral or emotional texture comes from **player choices**, not authored
  backstory.

## Starting conditions

- Starts with **nothing**, then eventually obtains **one rusty, low-status
  sword** (see acquisition below and `../Sword/`).
- **Completely illiterate** — see `../Mechanics/literacy-system.md`.
- The rusty sword becomes the only weapon the player can truly afford / use /
  rely on, creating **mutual desperation** between player and sword.

## How the sword is acquired (not destiny)

The sword's entry into the player's inventory is **practical, not destiny-based**:

- Someone else finds the rusted sword **offscreen** during quarantine cleanup,
  corpse search, confiscation, or breach response.
- They touch it, hear a private internal voice, and **assume it is a hivemind
  vector/artifact** (a plausible mistake — see
  `../Lore/sword-girl-backstory.md`).
- The hero is already quarantined / treated as expendable, so they **force the
  sword into his hands** to test him, punish him, or dump the danger onto him.
- The sword immediately speaks privately: *"Say nothing."*
- A quarantine breach escalates. The player has **no other weapon**. The sword
  enters inventory **by necessity.** From that point, progress requires it.

The first moment is **coercive for both parties**: the hero doesn't choose the
sword, and the sword doesn't choose the hero. They are forced together.

Good first private sword lines: *"Say nothing." / "This is not consent." /
"Another forced hand." / "I am not the choir." / "Do not call me many. I am one."*
(The last two distinguish the sword from the hivemind.)

> The starting relationship is not destiny. It is forced custody under emergency
> conditions. The hero is not the sword's owner. The sword is not his chosen
> weapon. They are both trapped in the same bad system.
