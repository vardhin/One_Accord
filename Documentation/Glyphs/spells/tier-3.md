# Tier 3 — Three-Slot Spells

> One hundred (100) spells — valid **three**-slot identities made from element sigils plus optional/required modifiers. Modifiers cannot stand alone; two or more element sigils require at least one modifier.
> Repeats are allowed in play (two Heats = a hotter Heat) but do **not** make a new spell — they only shift stat balance, so duplicates are not separate entries. See `../generate_spells.py` and `../glyph-system.md`.

Fill each entry over time: a name, what it does, its **class** (loan / kill / utility), energy cost, and any other key info. We are NOT authoring everything at once — append effects as they are discovered.
