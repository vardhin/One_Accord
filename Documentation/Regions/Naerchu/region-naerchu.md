# Naerchu — The Training Camp (the world's reproduction + training engine)

> **[promoted from stub]** — seeded from the catalogue (`../region-set.md` #7); now
> load-bearing. Naerchu is **not just a militia school** — it is the world's **single
> demographic institution**: training, mating, and the *permission to reproduce* all
> run through it. Its full role is specified in
> **`../../Mechanics/demographic-and-food-system.md`**; this doc is the place-level view.
>
> **Name:** **Naerchu** (the camp). Earlier "the Training Camp" (its function on the map).

## The three jobs (see the demographic system doc)

1. **Raising-to-hardness.** Every child from **every settlement** arrives at **age 6**
   and returns ~**18** trained. Childhood ends here.
2. **Mating.** The world's **entire mating-age cohort** is here; pairing happens here
   (wedlocks, communions, live-ins) and continues **until menopause.**
3. **Regulation — the hard gate.** Naerchu **enforces who may reproduce**: only couples
   who can show a **sustaining slot** (trade lineage / productive post / capacity to
   feed a child) are permitted children. This holds the population to the food supply.

> Take Naerchu and you take the future. It is the **valve on the species** — the highest
> strategic value on the human side.

## Food: pure consumer

Everyone here is training-age, so Naerchu **grows no food** and **caps at ~50 people.**
It is **100% fed by the ownerless rotating food caravan** (grain from Magizhee, protein
from Belgond — `../../Mechanics/demographic-and-food-system.md`). Cut its food and the
whole next generation starves: Naerchu is the food backbone's most critical node.

- **Tier / heat:** *mid* · cool-to-mid.
- **One line:** the place that does nothing but **harden people** — where Vendur (and
  likely other hubs) send their teenagers to mature. A rite-of-passage region: kids
  leave home soft and come back adults, or don't come back.

## Why it must exist

At band scale **no settlement can afford to train fighters at home** — every adult is a
working household (`../world-structure.md`: no slack). And in a world with no slack,
**reproduction itself must be regulated** so it never outruns the food. Both needs are
met by **one** institution: a place that hardens every child, pairs the mating-age
cohort, and gates who may have children. That is Naerchu.

## Lore role (pinned)

The **world's demographic engine** — not one town's militia school. Every settlement
**sends its children here at 6** and receives back **trained adults posted out as
guards** (lineage-posted home where possible). Emotionally **where childhood ends** —
*and* where adulthood, pairing, and the permission to reproduce all begin. The natural
**union-system hub** the whole map is tied to, since every region depends on it for its
next generation (`../../Mechanics/demographic-and-food-system.md`,
`../../Mechanics/village-and-union-system.md`).

## Map role (pinned)

**NE of Magizhee** (top-right of the human pocket), the safe eastern early route. Tied
to **every** settlement (it raises everyone's children), and to **Magizhee + Belgond**
specifically for its food (the rotating caravan). If visited first, the camp can send
the player to Magizhee/Vendur for supplies; its food dependence routes the player back.
