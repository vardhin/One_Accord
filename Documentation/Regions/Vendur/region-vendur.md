# Vendur — Region Overview

> Full design (factions, buildings, the dungeon, the homecoming arc as beats) lives in
> `vendur-detail.md`. This file is the overview.
>
> The sword-girl's **birthplace estate** — now a living **trade hub
> built on her grave.** The map's one crowded place, and the region where her arc
> gears from Witness → Partner. A little far from **Magizhee**, so the
> player carries her *home* without knowing it.

## What it was

The fortified noble estate of the **sword-girl's father**, who controlled the
**silver trade** (see `../../Lore/sword-girl-backstory.md`). Walled, silver-stocked,
with a **clock tower, scribes, old manuscripts, and a house seal** — the
best-defended real estate on the map. It died in the **staged plague breach** the
cleric exploited to force the soul-sword ritual. She was made into the sword here.

## What it is now

Nobody abandons the one place with working silver boundaries. Squatters
**reclaimed and renovated** it. Today it is a **trade hub** — the barter zone where
the scattered, food-self-sufficient survivor islands meet to swap the things
self-sufficiency can't produce alone: **silver, salvaged tools, maintenance
knowledge, seed varieties, medicine, blueprints, skills.**

- The map's **one relatively crowded, almost-warm place** — bustle, haggling,
  ~13 trader households under the safest walls around. After the stillness of
  everywhere else, it's a relief. Then the sword goes silent.
- **Home of the full forge / blacksmith** — the upgrade chain past the starter
  settlement's Forge L0→1 (see `../../Mechanics/forge-and-upgrades.md`).
- **Why silver matters here mechanically:** silver perma-kills **dumb husks**
  quietly, without summoning hive attention (see
  `../../Mechanics/kill-resolution.md`). Whoever controls silver controls *quiet*
  survival — which is exactly why a silver-trade estate became the trade hub.

## The horror: recognition under noise

Vendur's horror is **personal and quiet**, not environmental. The sword
**recognizes it.** The clock tower is hers. The **house seal over the gate** is now
a trade-guild mark — nobody alive knows what it meant. The new owners renovated
over her family's death.

> "They hung washing in my mother's solar."

This is the **collapsed object-meaning theme** (`../../Lore/setting-and-tech-level.md`)
turned on *her*: her world's meanings, erased and repurposed by strangers who mean
no harm. The pilgrimage payoff is **recognition under noise**, not the silence of a
ruin.

## The room underneath

Because she was *made* here, the **cleric's ritual / research room still exists** —
walled off, forgotten, or superstitiously avoided by the current townsfolk ("the
bad room"). It is the **sealed dungeon beneath the trade town**, and it holds the
truth she has been chasing: the cleric's research, the real nature of the rite, the
evidence to restore her name.

> Vendur is a **town on top of a wound.**

## Shape & population (see `vendur-detail.md`)

Vendur is an old-style **hillfort** — earth ramparts and wooden pikes — read as **two
coupled places under one name**: an **upper fort** (the estate bones — clock/watch
tower, silver vault, forge, "the bad room") and a **lower trader town** down the slope
(market, caravan yard, daily life, more exposed). About **13 trader households / 55–60
souls**, of whom **4 elderly**; ~4 households hold the exposed edge. **One faction (the
traders); power is functions held by named people, not blocs** — band sociology, not a
city (`../world-structure.md`). It trades — and **sends its children** to mature — along
a caravan tie to a separate **militia / coming-of-age settlement** (`../region-set.md`).

## Role in the game

- The **sword's region** — the one place tied directly to her origin. Her arc gears
  from **Witness → Partner** here (see `../../Lore/sword-girl-backstory.md`).
- Materially the region that makes the player **stronger** (full forge, silver
  supply) — at the cost of walking the sword through her own murdered home.
- The **handoff** (someone shoving the sword onto the player, assuming it's a hive
  vector — see `../../Player/player-premise.md`) happens **elsewhere**; over the
  rust-years she drifted away from home. The player carries her back.

## Hive-concentration character

- **Low baseline, but volatile in crowds.** A living trade hub is the one place
  husks-among-the-living is most plausible and most dangerous — and concentrated
  killing in a crowd triggers attention fast (see
  `../../Mechanics/hive-concentration.md`).
- **Threshold-matter look** (if driven past threshold): **tarnished silver and
  stopped clockwork** — possessed locks, halted gears, the clock tower going wrong.
