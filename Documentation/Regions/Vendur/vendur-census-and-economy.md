> The **buildable layer** of Vendur, derived **top-down** (services → manpower →
> families → headcount) per the world engine in
> `../../Mechanics/demographic-and-food-system.md`. `region-vendur.md` is the overview

## 1. Services Vendur must provide (the top-down root)

Vendur exists to be the **silver + trade hub** and to survive while doing it. That
demands:

- **Silver supply + boundary upkeep** — its identity; quiet survival.
- **Forge (L1→2)** — repair, sharpen, plain blades, temper.
- **Trade / barter + caravan operation** — the lines to Belgond, Teling, Velduri.
- **Defense** — it holds real value, so it must be **heavily guarded.**
- **Healing / plague-watch.**
- **Records / timekeeping** — the clock tower, the old manuscripts.
- **Food (partial)** — kitchen-scale farming + chickens; the rest is imported (§5).
- **Feeding & housing its own people** — a mess, storage, homes, barracks.

Manpower is derived from these services below; **headcount is the output.**

---

## 2. The physical town: a two-tier mud hill

Vendur is built on an **artificial mud hill** raised for security — a true hillfort.
**Two tiers, mechanically distinct:**

- **Hilltop = the market + the people-you-can't-lose** (the trades, their families,
  the records, the food store). Defensible high ground.
- **Foot of the hill = defense + bulk storage** (the guards, the barracks, the great
  stone storeroom). The ring that protects the top.

Everything below is placed on one tier or the other.

## 5. The economy — how Vendur feeds and pays itself

### Food: Vendur is NOT fully self-fed

Vendur has **~83 mouths** and roughly **9–13 effective farmers** (the 9 wives + some
elderly help). At ~2–4 mouths per farmer that covers maybe **~30–45 people** — **well
short of 83.** So Vendur runs a **food deficit by design**, covered by:

- **Belgond** — game/deer meat, hide, mushrooms, berries (protein and wild food) via
  Vendur's **regular trade caravan.**
- **The ownerless rotating food caravan** — tops up the staple-grain shortfall from
  **Magizhee's** surplus (`../../Mechanics/demographic-and-food-system.md`).
- **Its own** terraces + chickens cover the rest.

> Vendur trades its way to a full belly. Its silver and craft buy the food its hill
> can't grow — which is *why a silver-trade hub exists here at all.*

### Trade: the silver hub

Vendur is "the silver place" because **~80% of the map's silver circulates through
it** (worked into tools, ward-lines, coins — not a hoard; `../region-set.md` #11).
Silver perma-kills dumb husks **quietly** (`../../Mechanics/kill-resolution.md`), so
whoever moves silver controls quiet survival.

**The trade web (each line a dependency and a quest hook):**

| Partner | Vendur sends | Vendur receives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Belgond** | finished tools, silver/wards, finished leather, **militia-for-hire** | game meat, hide, timber, stone, mushrooms, berries |
| **Teling** | trade goods, silver | masterwork smithing, glyph-embedding (forge **L3→4**) |
| **Velduri** | *rarely* — trade goods | pure silver (rare), elite goods |
| **Magizhee / Belgond** (via rotating caravan) | loads + escorts the ownerless food caravan | grain (staples) |

### The cost structure (why the hub must stay alive)

Vendur **pays out** continuously: it **imports ~29 of its ~44 guards** (paid in
silver/goods) and **imports most of its food.** It **pays this** with silver supply,
forge work, finished leather, and brokerage. **Stop the caravans and Vendur cannot
defend or feed itself** — the hub isn't a luxury, it's life support. This is the
player's leverage and the hive's target.

## 6. The named draw on the 40-cap (~7)

Of ~83 residents, only the **function-holders** become **named** NPCs
(`../../Npcs/npc-tiers.md`); the rest are **Tier-3 ambient.** Names deferred; roles
pinned:

1. **Silver-keeper** (lead silversmith) — silver supply, boundary policy; the loud
   "the talking sword is a hive vector" voice (`../../Player/player-premise.md`).
2. **The blacksmith** — forge L1→2; points the player to **Teling** for L3→4.
3. **Remembering elder A** (a family elder) — memory, old keys, the sword's living link.
4. **Remembering elder B** — half-knows "the bad room"; the descent gate.
5. **Head trader** (of the 4) — market floor, caravan dispatch, the union/economy contact.
6. **The healer** — mercy calls, quarantine, the fever ledger.
7. **Guard captain** (foot) — speaks for the posted guards; defense/logistics contact.

> ~7 named for the map's densest hub; the ~76 others (wives, guards, elders, kids) are
> ambient population — counted for the economy, not against the 40.

## 7. Failure modes (what "no slack" means in play)

- **Lose the silver-keeper** → boundary-upkeep knowledge at risk; quiet survival degrades.
- **Lose the foot / a watchtower line** → the hilltop's value is exposed.
- **Lose a Belgond caravan** → protein + raw materials stall; the food deficit bites.
- **Lose access to the rotating caravan** → grain shortfall; Vendur starves slowly.
- **Lose posted guards** (Naerchu can't supply) → defense thins with no local replacement.

Each is a **drama lever** (`../../Mechanics/village-and-union-system.md`), now grounded
in real numbers.
