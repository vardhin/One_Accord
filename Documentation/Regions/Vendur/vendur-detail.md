# Vendur — Full Region Design

> The first **fully-authored** region. Builds on `region-vendur.md` (the overview —
> what it was, the horror, the room underneath) with the things a buildable region
> needs: **who runs it now, the factions, the building layout, the dungeon, and the
> sword's homecoming arc as playable beats.** Vendur is the map's central dense hub and
> the place the sword's knowledge-quest sends the player first
> (`../region-set.md`, `../../Lore/premise-and-the-one-accord.md`).

---

## What Vendur is, in one line

A **diminished trade hub built on the sword-girl's murdered home** — the one
relatively warm, crowded, defensible place on the map, where the player gets the full
forge and silver supply *while* walking the sword through her own grave. It is a
**town on top of a wound** (`region-vendur.md`).

## The stalemate, locally

Vendur is the clearest expression of the **stalemate frame** (`../../Lore/premise-and-the-one-accord.md`):
it is the closest thing to a *functioning* society left, and it is **completely stuck.**
The silver boundaries hold, trade happens, nobody starves — and it **cannot grow.** It
can't expand its walls (the husk owns everything past them), can't take in many more
mouths (no slack — `../world-structure.md`), can't resolve who really owns the place.
It is **prosperous by the standards of a dead world and frozen solid.** The player
arriving with the sword is the first new variable in a generation.

---

## Who runs Vendur now: the three claimants

Nobody legitimately owns Vendur — squatters reclaimed a dead noble estate
(`region-vendur.md`). So power is a **standoff between three groups**, none strong
enough to rule alone, none willing to leave. This is the region's **FNV-style faction
problem** — but framed as **stalemate, not war**: they are locked, not fighting.

### 1. The Boundary Wardens — *who hold the silver*

The faction that controls the **silver stores and the boundary maintenance.** Because
silver is *quiet* survival (`../../Mechanics/kill-resolution.md`), whoever maintains the
silver lines effectively controls who lives — so the Wardens are the closest thing to a
government. **Conservative, hoarding, legitimacy-obsessed.** They renovated the estate
and most fear change because change risks the boundary.

- **Controls:** the gatehouse, the silver vault, boundary upkeep, quarantine.
- **Wants:** stability above all. Sees the talking sword as a **boundary risk** (a
  possible hive vector) — a natural source of the "shove the sword onto a stranger"
  handoff suspicion (`../../Player/player-premise.md`).

### 2. The Free Barter / the trade-guild — *who run the market*

The merchants, brokers, and caravan people who make Vendur a **hub** — they moved the
seed-varieties, tools, medicine, and maintenance knowledge between the survivor islands
(`region-vendur.md`). **Cosmopolitan, pragmatic, rootless.** They hung their guild mark
over the old house seal without knowing what it meant — the **collapsed-object-meaning
theme** turned on the sword's family.

- **Controls:** the market, the storehouse allocation, outside contacts/caravans.
- **Wants:** open flow, more trade, fewer rules. Clashes with the Wardens' hoarding.
  Indifferent to the sword's history — which is its own kind of wound for her.

### 3. The Old Blood / remnant retainers — *who remember*

A small, fading group descended from (or once serving) the **original estate** — the
only people with any memory of what Vendur *was*. Diminished, half-superstitious, the
ones who avoid **"the bad room"** (the cleric's chamber, `region-vendur.md`). They have
no real power, but they are the **sword's only living link** to her own past.

- **Controls:** almost nothing — but they hold **memory, old keys, and access to the
  avoided places.**
- **Wants:** to be remembered / restored; quietly resent the squatters. The sword's
  homecoming is mostly *their* story too.

> The three-way lock is the quest engine: the player (with the sword's knowledge and
> the world's only new leverage) is the **first thing that can move any of them.**

## The dungeon: the cleric's room beneath the town

The **sealed research chamber** where she was made (`region-vendur.md`) is Vendur's
dungeon and the **payload of the homecoming questline:**

- It is **walled off / superstitiously avoided** ("the bad room"); the Old Blood know
  where it is, the Wardens don't want it opened (boundary fear), the Barter folk don't
  care.
- It holds the **cleric's research, the real nature of the rite, and the evidence to
  restore her name** — the things her main arc has been chasing
  (`../../Lore/sword-girl-backstory.md`).
- In light of the new soul-economy (`../../Lore/the-deities-and-the-soul-economy.md`),
  the room can also seed the bigger reveal: the cleric was groping at **soul-binding**
  and the **deity-lent nature of glyphs** without understanding it — a first thread of
  "glyphs are gods" that the underground sanctum (`../region-set.md` #4) later pays off.
- **It is hot.** A place where a soul was coerced into a blade is a place the hive can
  press on — concentration spikes here (`../../Mechanics/hive-concentration.md`), and a
  **soul-duel** (`../../Sword/soul-duels.md`) is a plausible climax for the descent.

## The sword's homecoming arc (playable beats)

Vendur is where her arc **gears from Witness → Partner** (`../../Lore/sword-girl-backstory.md`).
Authored as beats, not a cutscene:

1. **Recognition under noise.** She realizes *where she is* amid the bustle — the clock
   tower is hers, the seal over the gate is her house mark turned trade logo. The
   horror is **personal and quiet** (`region-vendur.md`): *"They hung washing in my
   mother's solar."* (Drives Resonance/Trust beats — `../../Sword/`.)
2. **The strangers who mean no harm.** The Barter guild's indifference and the Wardens'
   suspicion both wound her differently. The player choosing how to navigate the
   factions *for her* is the first real Partner act.
3. **The Old Blood door.** Reaching the people who remember — and through them, the keys
   to "the bad room." Earns the descent.
4. **The descent / the truth.** The dungeon: the cleric's research, the evidence, the
   soul-binding seed. The truth of her making, finally hers.
5. **What she does with it.** Restore her name publicly? Keep it? Use the leverage to
   break the three-way faction lock? This is where the **player makes her world bigger**
   (`../../Lore/sword-girl-backstory.md`) — or doesn't.

## Buildings & facilities

Vendur is dense, so it carries the **full facility set** (`../../Npcs/system-npc-roles.md`),
upgraded past the starter settlement's bare versions:

| Facility | Vendur version | Faction tie |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Forge** | The **full forge** (L0→4 chain, `../../Mechanics/forge-and-upgrades.md`) — the map's real blacksmith | Free Barter (smith is a trade asset) |
| **Silver vault & boundary** | The estate's working silver lines + store | Boundary Wardens |
| **Gatehouse** | Hardened access control + quarantine (1–2 capacity, `../world-structure.md`) | Boundary Wardens |
| **Market / storehouse** | The barter floor; allocation of irreplaceables | Free Barter |
| **Archive / decoder** | Old manuscripts, scribes, the clock-tower records | Old Blood / contested |
| **Infirmary** | Healer + plague-watcher / Checkup | shared |
| **The clock tower** | Diegetic landmark; hers; possible bell-code comms node | Old Blood memory |
| **"The bad room"** | The sealed dungeon (above) | avoided by all |

> **Threshold-matter look** if driven hot (`../../Mechanics/hive-concentration.md`):
> **tarnished silver and stopped clockwork** — possessed locks, halted gears, the clock
> tower going wrong. Vendur over-fought becomes a town of frozen, breathing machinery.

## Role on the spine

- **Mid-spine dense hub** (`../connectivity-map.md`): a power spike (full forge + silver)
  and the sword's emotional pivot, reached through at least one corridor from the starter
  settlement. The player carries her **home without knowing it.**
- **The handoff happens elsewhere** (`region-vendur.md`); the player *brings* her back
  over the rust-years' drift.
- Branch point: from Vendur the spine continues toward the hotter lore regions (Rosetta
  ruins → sanctum → mountain).
