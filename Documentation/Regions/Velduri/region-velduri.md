## Why it must exist

Silver is the survival/anti-husk metal, and the map's silver is **~80% Vendur's, but
all in circulation.** The other **~20% is held as an actual pure reserve by Velduri** —
the only true silver stockpile on the map. That reserve is what lets them stay rich,
safe, and aloof.

## Lore role (pinned)

**Old elite who fled the collapse** — descendants of a pre-collapse wealthy house who
withdrew to a defensible, silver-rich spot and rode out the famine in comfort. A
**third kind of survivor** (old order), distinct from the war-remnants
(Vengarz/Teling) and the working bands (Vendur/Belgond). Security is **terrain + pure
silver, not a hillfort** — husks don't arrive from the mountain edges
(`../world-structure.md`). Depends on the nearby **medicine hermit** (#13) for healing.

## Map role (pinned)

**South of Vendur**, deeper into the southern mountain range, human-pocket side — a
**mountain-defended** enclave off the main spine. Extends the southern cluster:
Magizhee → Vendur → (SE) Teling / (S) Velduri. A **rare** Vendur client. Name fossil:
**Velduri** (from *velli*, silver — echoing Vendur's `vendi+ooru`).

## Endgame hook (pinned)

Courting Velduri's alliance unlocks **elite soldiers** (their private retainers) and
**pure-silver access for endgame weapon supply** — a late-game union objective feeding
the final war (`../../Mechanics/village-and-union-system.md`,
`../../Mechanics/hivemind-threads.md`).
