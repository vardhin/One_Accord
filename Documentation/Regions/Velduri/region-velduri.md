  remnants holding the map's only **pure, uncirculated silver reserve**.

## Why it must exist

Silver is the survival/anti-husk metal, and the map's silver is **~80% Vendur's, but
all in circulation.** The other **~20% is held as an actual pure reserve by Velduri** —
the only true silver stockpile on the map. That reserve is what lets them stay rich,
safe, and aloof.

## Lore role (pinned)

**Old elite who fled the collapse** — descendants of a pre-collapse wealthy house who
withdrew to a defensible, silver-rich spot and rode out the famine in comfort. A
**third kind of survivor** (old order), distinct from the war-remnants
(Vengarz/Teling) and the working bands (Vendur/Belgond). Security is **terrain + pure
silver, not a hillfort** — husks don't arrive from the mountain edges
(`../world-structure.md`). Depends on the nearby **medicine hermit** (#13) for healing.

## Map role (pinned)

**South of Vendur**, deeper into the southern mountain range, human-pocket side — a
**mountain-defended** enclave off the main spine. Extends the southern cluster:
Magizhee → Vendur → (SE) Teling / (S) Velduri. A **rare** Vendur client. Name fossil:
**Velduri** (from *velli*, silver — echoing Vendur's `vendi+ooru`).

## Endgame hook (pinned)

Courting Velduri's alliance unlocks **elite soldiers** (their private retainers) and
**pure-silver access for endgame weapon supply** — a late-game union objective feeding
the final war (`../../Mechanics/village-and-union-system.md`,
`../../Mechanics/hivemind-threads.md`).

## Census (~64 present; OFF the Naerchu grid)

Velduri is **demographically self-contained** — the deliberate exception to the world
system (`../../Mechanics/demographic-and-food-system.md`). It runs its own closed cycle:
**arranged endogamous marriage** (elders pick their children's mates — they refuse
Naerchu's *unselected* mating), **in-house training**, and **veteran guards**.

| Group | Count | Role / living quarters |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| **Silver-elite couples** | **12** (6 couples) | The house itself; all live in **one big shared mansion.** |
| **Elderly** | **6** | The old-order memory; mind the young children. |
| **Servants** | **8** | 4 butlers, 2 cooks, 2 maids — the elite don't do their own labor. |
| **Veteran elite guards** | **25** | Picked from Naerchu **long ago — now ~40 each, but tough.** Mostly a **deterrent** (a display of force flanking the silver, to scare off anyone eyeing it). Housed in **two mini-mansions on either side** of the main one. |
| **Instructor** | **1** | Trains Velduri's own children in-house (since they don't go to Naerchu) — into the **next generation of elite-class fighters.** |
| **Children** | **12** | **4 under-6** + **~8 aged 6–17** — **all raised and trained at home.** None at Naerchu. The kids grow as powerful as the elite guards (the closed martial lineage). |
| **Present total** | **~64** | Self-contained; the only outside ties are the **medicine hermit** (#13, healing) and the **rotating caravan** (goods on request). |

> **Why it conserves trivially:** Velduri **takes nothing from and gives nothing to**
> Naerchu's 34 trainees — it is off the grid. Its only renewal is internal (the
> instructor raises kids into guards); it **rarely re-hires** veterans from Naerchu.
> This is the worldbuilding payoff of an insular elite: in a world organized around one
> shared reproduction engine, *opting out* is itself the ultimate statement of wealth
> and separateness. **Healers are external by design** (the medicine hermit), so Velduri
> has no in-house healer — a real dependency the alliance arc can exploit.
