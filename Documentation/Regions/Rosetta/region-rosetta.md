## Why it must exist

This is **where the world ended.** The sword's knowledge-quest needs what was *left
behind* here (the old research region itself), not the famous tome. Right-side lore,
especially Rosetta, is what teaches the player to reinterpret the mountain trail and
return to the **Sword Sanctum** (`../connectivity-map.md`).

## Lore role (pinned)

The old research region: once full of hundreds of researchers, field camps, glyph
halls, containment attempts, failed expeditions, decade-old camping sites. Dense
because the **site** is dense, not because people live there now. The hive's attention
bends here because it **arrived from here** and may be trying to remove or bypass the
old hero's seal (`../connectivity-map.md` hive-pressure model).

## Map role (pinned)

Across the water, **north of Vengarz Hold** and before the Silent Core (north of
Rosetta); diagonal-opposite Magizhee on the design diagonal (start/living procedure ⇄
origin-wound/seal mystery). The **Vengarz** (#3b) are the living link to its routes and
send recovery sorties into it. **Digzarr** (#15) sits in the same hive-side orbit.
