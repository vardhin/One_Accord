## Why it must exist

The **forgotten-but-living deities** (`../../Lore/the-deities-and-the-soul-economy.md`)
need a place their indirect hand is legible — a divine maze/archive of the ~10. It
holds the **first Light-glyph knowledge** — the deities' most overt lent power, earned
by **navigating/surviving the labyrinth.**

## Lore role (pinned)

Where the **"glyphs are gods" truth** first surfaces: humans think it's decoded
technique; here the sword realizes otherwise. Not a tomb of sealed gods — a
**channel-point of living, abstaining ones**, shaped as a maze that tests the seeker.

## Map role (pinned)

Across the water, on the **second path out of Arghanzza** (the one that does *not* lead
to Vengarz Hold). It hangs off **Arghanzza**, not Rosetta. Gated by a traversal/tool
gate on the Arghanzza branch, then by the maze itself.
