# Vengarz Hold — Mobile Stronghold Near Rosetta

> A mid-size, mobile fortified camp south of Rosetta, reached from Arghanzza via the
> puzzle route. Technically a **camp**; the Vengarz call it a **Hold.** Outsiders may
> mistake them for scavengers, but the Vengarz are the remnant of an old anti-hive
> force that fought beside the hero during the seal era.

## Identity

**Vengarz** is the surviving name of an old war force: one of the older houses /
legionary remnants that fought the hive from the beginning, well before the
present stalemate and before the cultural memory of the **Silver Anarchy** settled
into rumor.

They are not opportunistic ruin-looters. They are a diminished fighting order with
inherited doctrine:

- Glyphed silver weapons.
- Old Rosetta route knowledge.
- Field glyph procedures they preserve even when they no longer fully understand
  the theory.
- Recovery sorties into Rosetta, usually in disciplined bands of about six.
- A culture of duty to the old hero's unfinished war.

## Scale

Mid-size zone, roughly **20 adults**, all hardened. No soft village structure, no
children as the default image, no broad civilian economy. The camp should feel like
a war remnant and expeditionary base, not a normal settlement.

Likely facilities:

- Sleeping tents / dorm tents.
- Cookery / ration tent.
- Training yard.
- Glyph hall / map tent / archive chests.
- Glyph-smith + silver-smith wagon.
- Armory wagon.
- Sortie gate.
- Collapsible watch platforms.
- Portable boundary lines.

## Why it moves

Vengarz cannot survive as a stone fort or mud hillfort. The hive is actively trying
to interfere with Rosetta; anything too fixed gives it time to learn the ground,
the walls, the sleeping routes, the wells, and the weak habits.

Their "stronghold" is therefore mobile:

- Heavy tents.
- Wooden palisades.
- Wagon-walls.
- Collapsible towers.
- Silver-thread boundary ropes.
- Portable glyph stakes.
- Rotating perimeter fires.
- Packed archive chests that can move at short notice.

The Vengarz are a stronghold by **discipline**, not architecture.

## Relationship to Rosetta

Rosetta itself is a **dense hazard/lore region** because it once held hundreds of
researchers: old camps, research halls, field stations, glyph archives,
containment attempts, and failed expeditions. The Vengarz Hold is the human
foothold near it, not inside it.

Route cluster (Arghanzza forks; the Sanctum hangs off the **other** branch):

```text
[Arghanzza] ──puzzle──> [Vengarz Hold] ──N──> [Rosetta Ruins] ──N──> [Silent Core]
     └──────────── second path ──────────> [Pathali / Divine Labyrinth]
```

The structure mirrors the useful design function of Fallout 1's Brotherhood /
Glow-style threshold: a disciplined knowledge-war faction tests the player by
sending them toward an old-world danger zone. Here, the danger is Rosetta: the
place where the hive first entered and where the old research may still hold
answers about glyphs and the seal.

## Quest function

Early Vengarz state:

- Suspicious of outsiders.
- Better armed than most survivors, but under constant pressure.
- Still performing dangerous recovery sorties into Rosetta.
- Preserving old glyph doctrine as procedure more than understanding.
- Unable to build a permanent base because the hive learns fixed places.

Player/sword intervention:

- The sword recognizes old defensive principles in their doctrine.
- Recovered Rosetta research explains why some procedures work.
- New / old tech trees unlock: better glyph stakes, silver boundary patterns,
  stable anti-hive ground wards, improved watch rotations, safer sortie prep.
- The camp can become a more stable **Vengarz Hold** without losing its rough,
  expeditionary identity.

## Naming note

Names in this world are language fossils, like **Vendur** from `vendi + ooru`.
**Vengarz** should feel like an eroded war-name, not a clean modern compound. It
echoes silver / guard / garrison without needing to explain itself as a direct
translation.

Open: the deeper historical root of **Vengarz** — old house name, legion banner,
or war-era title.
