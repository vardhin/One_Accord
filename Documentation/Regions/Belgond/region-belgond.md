  trade hub *make sense.*

## Why it must exist

The trade economy demands a source. Vendur crafts and keeps silver but sits in a
hillfort basin and **cannot extract** stone, timber, hide, or game at scale. Belgond
is the producer end of the economic triangle: **Belgond (raw) ↔ Vendur
(forge/silver/leather) ↔ Naerchu / Training Camp (fighters-for-hire).** Food-independent (hunt +
forage), so unlike most of the map it does **not fear hunger** — it fears losing
tools, wards, and bodies.

## Shape (pinned)

One **main colony of homes** — dense, warm, defensible core — with **worksites
scattered ~5 minutes out** (quarry, wood-lots, hunting ranges, mushroom grounds).
People **sleep together, work apart**: dawn muster, scatter to the sites, regroup at
dusk. A sparse *labor* footprint over a dense *living* footprint.

## Census (~37 present; conserves into the world table)

Derived top-down (`../../Mechanics/demographic-and-food-system.md` §5–6). Belgond is
**structurally different** from the trade towns: its **work-militia ARE its workforce.**

| Group | Count | Role |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| **Work-cum-militia** | **24** (12 on shift) | **The foragers, miners, hunters, and timber crews** — they **extract on-shift and guard the scattered worksites the rest** (`../../Mechanics/demographic-and-food-system.md` §4). No separate "worker" count: the guards *are* the labor. |
| **Headman / foreman** | **1** | Organizes the worksites, the caravan loads, the dawn muster/dusk regroup. |
| **Tool-smith** | **1** | Repairs the constantly-breaking extraction tools (picks, saws, blades). |
| **Elderly** | **6** | Cook for the colony, mind the under-6 kids. |
| **Children (<6)** | **~5** | At home; sent to Naerchu at 6. |
| **Present total** | **~37** | Plus **~8 of Belgond's children away at Naerchu.** Its few **lineage couples** (headman, tool-smith, senior work-militia) hold most of those kids. |

> **Why so lean:** folding the workforce and the militia into one cohort is what lets a
> small, *scattered* colony both produce raw materials and defend perimeter it can't
> otherwise afford to ward (the colony is wallable; the worksites ~5 min out are not —
> see Shape). Belgond is dense by **activity**, thin by **headcount.**

## Map role (pinned)

**West of Magizhee,** partway up the western predator-mountain Sanctum road. Reached
by **armed caravan** through the big-cat / bear gauntlet (no lone traveler survives the
road — this is *why caravans exist*). The **last inhabited place** before the trail
climbs to the mountain Sword Sanctum (#5), so it naturally **stages** that late return.
