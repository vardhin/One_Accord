# Arghanzza — Traversal & Navigation Rules

> The forest is a rule-based labyrinth. The sword is the TRANSLATION LAYER, not
> the KEY — a clever player can cross without it.

## Three traversal pillars

- **Sense** — sword or player observation detects forest intent.
- **Anchor** — pyres, ash, old paths, and stones stabilize space.
- **Discipline** — silence, timing, route memory, careful movement prevent the
  hive from learning you.

## The sword is the translation layer, not the key

The sword is the **intended** traversal instrument, but **not an absolute key.**
Without it, a smart player can still cross by solving the environmental rules
manually.

- **With the sword:** warnings and hints — *"Stone." "Not that voice." "Wait for
  the exhale." "The mud is listening." "That tree was behind us."*
- **Without the sword:** infer the same things from moss, mud, breathing cycles,
  path loops, pyre behavior, old village layout, and the **poem** (see
  `old-npc-and-poem.md`).

## Three traversal modes

1. **Sword-guided** — safest and most intended. Sword senses hive attention,
   false paths, breathing trees, mud memory, hidden tree bodies.
2. **Manual** — player observes the rules and solves the labyrinth unaided.
3. **Brute-force / alternate** — fire, salt, ropes, companions, bells, hive
   intrusion, or other tools. Works, but has **costs.**

## Navigation rules

### The forest loops when it hears you

Talking, sprinting, metal noise, and fighting near **ear-stones** can **reset
paths.**

### The mud remembers you

Stepping in **hive-mud** records your movement/rhythm, **predicts routes, spawns
false paths,** and makes enemies anticipate you.

### The pines breathe in cycles

Move during **exhale.** Hold still during **inhale** — during inhale, roots
detect/grab movement.

### Pyres anchor true space

Correctly lit/cleansed pyres **stabilize** nearby paths. **Wrong** pyres can
awaken enemies or deepen loops.

### True paths follow old Arghanzza routines

Old village habits — children's paths, mill paths, funeral paths, well paths,
cottage orientation — can guide navigation.

## Hive-mud mechanics

Stepping in it: records weight/rhythm · enemies path better · false footprints
appear · whispers mimic recent NPC dialogue · sword cooperation/sensing can suffer
under high hive contact.

### Countering mud

Ash boots · walking on stones/logs · washing in streams · changing rhythm ·
moving during exhale · laying salt · using guide knowledge.

## Forest awareness variable

A region-wide **awareness** meter.

- **Increases:** sound near ear-stones · stepping in mud · killing loudly · wrong
  pyres · following whispers · bell/glyph noise · hive intrusion.
- **Decreases/stabilizes:** ash boots · correct pyres · silence · old paths ·
  sword guidance · careful timing.

### High awareness effects

More loops · more enemies · more false voices · sword stress · **false
sword-voice** · changed paths.

## Detection interaction

The sword's glow helps here — but **silent rocks dim it** and the hive may
**spoof** it under pressure. With the detection upgrade, crossing is easier;
without it, manual solving is required. (See `../../Sword/glow-and-detection.md`.)
