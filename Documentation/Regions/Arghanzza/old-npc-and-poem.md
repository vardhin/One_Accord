# Arghanzza — The Old NPC & The Poem

> The poem is not just flavor. It encodes traversal rules. An old NPC recites it.

## The reciter

The poem should be recited by an **old NPC** — one of the last people who
remembers the old, beautiful Arghanzza.

Candidates: charcoal burner · corpse-burner · blind woodcutter · failed pyre
keeper · hermit.

### What the old NPC can do

- Guide the player.
- Teach **pyre paths.**
- Know **old warnings.**
- Improve **ash/fire rites.**
- Connect to **forge fuel** (charcoal).
- Help **decode forest marks.**

(This NPC is part of the named-roster budget — see
`../../Npcs/core-named-roster.md`.)

## The poem

```text
--Arghanzza

Silent are the rocks, in the woods of crooked pines,
where ancient secrets whisper. Through whimpering twisted spines.

Silent are the rocks, in the woods of sunken ears,
of sprawling thorns that lean, their twisted forms conspire.

Screaming shreiking woes, of burning bleeding eyes,
lurking in the trunks, of crooked twisted pines.

Looming through the mists, ancient forms unseen,
in the midst of nights, through the paths of pyres,
in the woods of arghanzza
Where silent are the rocks,
In the woods of breathing pines,
The woods of arghanzza.
```

*(Spelling preserved as authored — "shreiking" is from the source.)*

## Poem → mechanics map

The poem doubles as a navigation primer. Each image encodes a rule (see
`traversal.md`):

| Poem image | Mechanic |
| --- | --- |
| **Silent rocks** | Avoid sound / listening stones |
| **Sunken ears** | Ear-shaped rocks detect sound (ear-stones) |
| **Burning bleeding eyes** | Fire reveals tree weak points / hidden eyes |
| **Paths of pyres** | Pyres anchor navigation |
| **Breathing pines** | Tree breath cycles (move on exhale) |

## Design intent

A player who **listens to the poem** and decodes it can navigate **without the
sword** — reinforcing "the sword is the translation layer, not the key." The poem
is the **manual-mode** counterpart to sword-guided traversal.
