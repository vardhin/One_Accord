- **Tier / heat:** *dense* · cool / safe.
- **One line:** the **making** counterpart to Vengarz's **doctrine** — where the
  **high craft** lives: glyph-embedding, silver wards, masterwork weapon bodies, and
  the soul-forge work on the sentient sword. The **L3→4 mastery gate**
  (`../../Mechanics/forge-and-upgrades.md`).

## Why it must exist

Vendur does **forge basics (L1→2)** but is "the silver place," not the master smith.
The lore needs somewhere the high craft actually lives. To take the sword past L2 the
player must reach Teling and likely earn the guild's trust (a threshold like Vengarz's).

## Lore role (pinned)

A hereditary **smith-guild + craft lineage + glyph-keepers** with **old-hero-legion
links** — they forged and embedded for the hero's war (his gear may have been **Teling
steel**). They are the *making* fragment of the old war effort as **Vengarz** is the
*fighting* fragment. Clannish — they keep their craft secrets, which is *why* they
stayed their own region instead of folding into Vendur. Their glyph-embedding knowledge
edges toward the **"glyphs are gods"** truth
(`../../Lore/the-deities-and-the-soul-economy.md`).

## Map role (pinned)

**SE of Vendur, human-pocket side** — mastery is reachable *without* crossing the
river, deepening the southern corridor (Magizhee → Vendur → Teling). A Vendur client
(Vendur trades Teling's masterwork output). Name fossil: **Teling** (from "Teling
steel," the old name for Telangana's wootz steel).
