- **Tier / heat:** *dense* · cool / safe.
- **One line:** the **making** counterpart to Vengarz's **doctrine** — where the
  **high craft** lives: glyph-embedding, silver wards, masterwork weapon bodies, and
  the soul-forge work on the sentient sword. The **L3→4 mastery gate**
  (`../../Mechanics/forge-and-upgrades.md`).

## Why it must exist

Vendur does **forge basics (L1→2)** but is "the silver place," not the master smith.
The lore needs somewhere the high craft actually lives. To take the sword past L2 the
player must reach Teling and likely earn the guild's trust (a threshold like Vengarz's).

## Lore role (pinned)

A hereditary **smith-guild + craft lineage + glyph-keepers** with **old-hero-legion
links** — they forged and embedded for the hero's war (his gear may have been **Teling
steel**). They are the *making* fragment of the old war effort as **Vengarz** is the
*fighting* fragment. Clannish — they keep their craft secrets, which is *why* they
stayed their own region instead of folding into Vendur. Their glyph-embedding knowledge
edges toward the **"glyphs are gods"** truth
(`../../Lore/the-deities-and-the-soul-economy.md`).

## Map role (pinned)

**SE of Vendur, human-pocket side** — mastery is reachable *without* crossing the
river, deepening the southern corridor (Magizhee → Vendur → Teling). A Vendur client
(Vendur trades Teling's masterwork output). Name fossil: **Teling** (from "Teling
steel," the old name for Telangana's wootz steel).

## Census (~48 present; the craft-guild model)

Derived top-down (`../../Mechanics/demographic-and-food-system.md` §5–6). Teling
inverts the usual shape: its **lineage layer is thick** (the masters *are* the town),
its guard layer is small-but-elite, and it **mostly raises its own children** to keep
the craft in-blood.

| Group | Count | Role |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| **Master craftsmen** | **10** | **6 silversmiths, 2 blacksmiths, 2 glyph masters** — the guild; the L3→4 mastery gate (`../../Mechanics/forge-and-upgrades.md`). Close-quartered (the craft needs proximity, not sprawl). |
| **Their wives** | **10** | Farm communally (the Vendur model — the craft is the men's lineage, the women feed the guild). |
| **Elite guards** | **16** (8 on shift) | **Top-notch** — the **best of the Naerchu-posted cohort**, assigned here because Teling is the **main weapons armory + building station**, the highest-value human-side site to defend. They rotate from the shared pool. |
| **Elderly** | **4** | Cook and maintain the place. |
| **Children** | **~10** | **Only ~20% (~2) go to Naerchu;** the **~80% (~8) are guild-raised at home** — apprenticed young, married within the lineage, never sent to unselected mating. This is the **clannish craft-secrecy** made demographic. |
| **Present total** | **~48** | Plus the ~2 away at Naerchu. |

> **Teling is a *partial* dissenter** (cf. Velduri's total opt-out): it keeps most kids
> in-guild to protect craft secrets and bloodline mastery, but still **draws its guards
> from Naerchu** (it takes the system's best, even as it withholds most of its own
> young). The 80% guild-raising is the mechanical reason Teling "stayed its own region
> instead of folding into Vendur" — secrecy isn't just attitude, it's how they
> reproduce. **No in-house healer** noted yet (resolve in the concept pass — likely a
> Vendur/medicine-hermit dependency).
