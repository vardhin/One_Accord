# Connectivity Map — Layout, Gates, and Route Spine

> Companion to `world-structure.md` (tiers) and
> `../Mechanics/hive-concentration.md` (heat). This doc is **structure, not
> content**: it fixes the map skeleton, the early route choices, the river
> threshold, the Arghanzza fork, and the late reinterpretation of the mountain
> Sanctum.
>
> **Visual source of truth:** `One_Accord_Map.png` (in this folder). Where prose
> and the drawn map ever disagree, the map wins and this doc is corrected to match.

---

## The physical map

The world is a square split diagonally by a **broad body of water** (the "river"
threshold), running roughly **west → lower-east**.

- The **lower-left** is the **human pocket**: drier grassland, more inhabited, sealed
  along its **western and southern edges by mountain ranges**. Husks do not arrive
  from those mountain edges.
- The **upper / across-the-water** half is the **hive side**: dense reclaimed
  forest, ruins, and hive-concentrated ground.
- The **south-west mountain** (inside the protective range, above the human pocket)
  holds the **Sword Sanctum**.
- The **top-right** holds the **Silent Core**, the densest hive-mind region.

The big symbolic diagonal is:

```text
Sword Sanctum / mountains / old resistance  ⇄  Silent Core / forest / hive mind
```

Magizhee and Rosetta form another design diagonal:

```text
Magizhee / start / living procedure  ⇄  Rosetta / origin wound / seal mystery
```

## Early-game routing — the Magizhee compass

The start is simple on purpose. **Magizhee is the hub.** From it, four directions,
each gated diegetically — readable world limits, not invisible walls:

```text
                    [Old Dock] — NORTH
                  (husk-infested; crossing point)
                          │  ← very tough husks early
                          │
[mountains] ── WEST ──[Magizhee]── EAST ──[Training Camp]
(big cats, bears;        │              (safe early route)
 way up to Sanctum)      │
                       SOUTH
                          │
                   [Vendur Militia]
                          │
                 [Vendur Hill Fort Market]
                   (safe early route)
```

- **North → Old Dock:** the ferry crossing, but **husk-infested.** Going straight
  north early throws the player at **very tough husks.** This is the river threshold
  *and* a fight.
- **West → mountains:** tough mountainous routes with **dangerous big cats and
  bears.** This is also the way up toward the **Sword Sanctum** — so it reads as
  "blocked early" terrain, not a path.
- **East → Training Camp:** the safe early direction. The coming-of-age settlement.
- **South → Vendur:** the other safe early direction — the **Vendur Militia** first,
  then up to the **Vendur Hill Fort Market** beyond it.

So the **two sane early routes are East (Training Camp) and South (Vendur)**; North
and West are self-evident danger early.

- **Training-Camp-first:** the camp can send the player to Vendur for supplies,
  repairs, grain, medicine, authority, or another practical need.
- **Vendur-first:** the sword suggests Vendur; Vendur opens the first dungeon and
  the sword's homecoming arc.

Vendur is where the early routes converge. It is still the first major hub and the
region where the player gains enough material and emotional context to understand
that the world is older than the local survival problem.

## Midgame river threshold — the ferry from the husk-held dock

The water is the true act boundary.

- The player cannot cross it freely at the start.
- Crossing is via a **ferry from the Old Dock** — but the **dock itself is
  husk-infested**, so reaching and holding the crossing is a midgame achievement, not
  a free move.
- The first crossing should feel like leaving the human-maintained half of the map
  and entering ground the hive has had time to re-grow.

## The Arghanzza fork — the hive side branches

After the ferry, a **short distance** lands the player at **Arghanzza**, the first
post-crossing threshold: wet, root-tangled, mangrove-like, possessed matter, and
dangerous in a different way from the left-side survival pocket.

**Arghanzza is the fork point of the hive side.** Two paths leave it:

```text
                                         ┌─ (puzzle route) ─▶ [Vengarz Hold] ─N─▶ [Rosetta] ─N─▶ [Silent Core]
[Old Dock] ──ferry──▶ [Arghanzza] ──────┤
                                         └─ (second path) ──▶ [Ancient Deity Sanctum]
```

- **Puzzle route → Vengarz Hold:** a **traversal/puzzle gate** out of Arghanzza
  leads to the Vengarz human foothold. From Vengarz Hold, **north** reaches
  **Rosetta**; **north** of Rosetta reaches the **Silent Core**. This is the main
  hive-side spine.
- **Second path → Ancient Deity Sanctum:** a different route out of Arghanzza
  reaches the deities' sanctum (first Light-glyph knowledge). It hangs off
  **Arghanzza**, not off Rosetta.

Vengarz Hold is the human foothold before Rosetta: a mid-size mobile palisade /
tent / wagon stronghold of old anti-hive fighters. Technically a camp; they call it
a **Hold.** They do not live in Rosetta because the hive is actively interfering
there.

## The Sanctum return

After Vendur, the player can find or hear of the **mountain trail** west/up toward
the **Sword Sanctum**. An early-level player is stopped by terrain, predators
(big cats, bears), traversal limits, or simple practical impossibility.

The trail's real meaning unlocks later. The player returns because they have found
enough lore to reinterpret it, not because an NPC plainly says, "there is a magic
sword on the mountain."

Primary sources for this reinterpretation:

- Right-side lore, especially **Rosetta**.
- Old people speaking indirectly about **Silver Anarchy**, magic, old weapons with
  names, or the mountain's historical terror.
- The sword's own partial, emotional, and possibly unreliable nudges.

The midgame return reveals the **true Sword Maiden** at the Sanctum. This makes the
mountain a late-reinterpreted place, not simply a far-away final node.

## Hive pressure model on the map

The old hero's seal prevents new living infection. Because of that, the hive has
lost much of its motivation to actively invade the lower-left human pocket. Its
attention bends toward **Rosetta**, the region where it arrived from, because that
is where it may find a way to remove or bypass the seal.

Human-pocket danger still exists:

- Runaway husks (the Old Dock is a standing example — a husk-held chokepoint inside
  the "safe" half).
- Low-level drift across roads or along water.
- Old husk pockets in ruins, caves, or bad corridors.
- Accidents that become catastrophic because only a few hundred humans remain.

So the human pocket is not "safe." It is simply not the hive's main strategic front.

## Gate types

1. **Natural boundary gates.** The water, the western/southern mountains (big cats,
   bears), and husk-heavy ground establish early map legibility.
2. **Heat gates.** Hotter regions punish early entry through baseline hive
   concentration (`../Mechanics/hive-concentration.md`).
3. **Traversal/tool gates.** The ferry crossing, the **Arghanzza→Vengarz puzzle
   route**, the mountain trail survival, and any later right-side tools.
4. **Story/lore gates.** The Sanctum return in particular should be unlocked by
   understanding, not just stats.
