> **Name:** **Vaelp** (the mountain sanctum). Earlier called "The Mountain Sword
> Sanctum."

- **Tier / heat:** *mini (late-gated)* · low physical edge / high story weight.
- **One line:** the hero's old route to the **true Soul Sword** — which **withdrew here
  and guards itself**, now as a **young sword maiden** with recent grief and new terms.

## Why it must exist

The **One Accord** payoff (`../../Lore/premise-and-the-one-accord.md`): where you can
bind to the true sword and must choose between her and your rusty one. The sanctum is
**untouched** (only the hero ever came). Its relationship to the ~10 deities is flagged
open.

## Lore role (pinned)

The guardian is **not** an old man but a **young maiden** whose loss is recent. Because
she is young, she **does not demand willing self-sacrifice** the way the old guardian
did — the terms have changed, and there are **new trails up** beyond the hero's single
path. She is real and obtainable.

## Map role (pinned)

The **SW mountain** above the human pocket, inside the protective range — reached
**west** out of Magizhee through predator-terrain, **beyond Belgond** (#8), the last
inhabited place before the trail climbs. Physically near the early region but
**story-gated to a late return**: the player can find the trail after Vendur, but it
stops an early player; right-side lore (especially **Rosetta**) teaches the
reinterpretation that lets them return (`../connectivity-map.md`).

## Census (1 — the sword maiden)

Vaelp has **one inhabitant: the true sword maiden** herself
(`../../Lore/premise-and-the-one-accord.md`). The sanctum is **untouched** — only the
hero ever came — so there is no settlement, no staff, no population. **1 NPC** (a
Tier-0-adjacent deep character given her story weight), wholly **off the Naerchu grid**
(she is not a survivor of the human system at all). The region's "population" is the
maiden + the empty sanctum + the ascent to her.
