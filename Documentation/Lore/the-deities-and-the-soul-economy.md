# The Deities & The Soul Economy

> The unifying metaphysics. Souls, deities, soul swords, glyphs, and the hive are all
> **one substance in one possession economy** — this doc is the spine they hang from.
> **Supersedes** the "the other divinities sealed him" framing in `ancient-history.md`
> (they did not seal him and are not themselves sealed — see §1) and grounds the
> `possession vs. partnership` theme (`../genre-and-themes.md`) in a concrete ontology.

---

## 1. There is one substance: **soul**

Everything that matters in this world's metaphysics is **soul**:

- **Deities are ascended souls** — not a separate order of being. They are souls that
  **ascended at the beginning of eons**: the oldest, vastest, most powerful souls there
  are. Human in origin, god in age and weight. There are **~10** of them.
- **A soul sword is a soul too** — a soul bound into a blade (the true sword: willingly;
  the rusty sword: by coercion — `sword-girl-backstory.md`).
- **The hive is a soul** — specifically a soul whose nature is to **occupy other souls.**
  He believes only his own selfhood is real and all else is his material/extension
  (`ancient-history.md`).

This is why **severance works.** A soul sword can sever a husk because it meets the
hive **soul-to-soul** — the same substance. Metal, fire, and silver only damage *bodies*
(`../Mechanics/kill-resolution.md`); only a soul can contest the hive's grip on a soul.

## 2. The hive is **Sauron**, not a plague

Reframe the threat: the hive is less "an infection" and more a **possessing will** in the
Sauron mold. He can **influence and ultimately take over anything with a soul-channel:**

- ordinary **people** (husks, Actives, the soft-infected — `../Mechanics/plague-and-infection.md`),
- the **soul swords** (yes — your sword can be contested),
- and even, in principle, **the deities themselves.**

That last point is the whole geopolitics of the setting (§3).

## 3. The deities are **forgotten, alive, and deliberately abstaining**

The other ~10 deities were **never sealed.** They are simply **forgotten** — and they
are **alive, watching, and choosing not to act.** Their abstention is **strategy, not
absence:**

> If the hive directly contacts a deity, there is a chance — even a slight one — that he
> **takes that deity over.** And the hive **taking even one deity means the hive wins
> outright.** A single ascended soul of that scale, occupied, ends everything.

So the deities will **not** directly interfere. Direct presence is the one move they
cannot risk. They are gods playing a game where stepping onto the board loses it.

### How they help anyway: glyphs (the indirect channel)

The deities **lend their power indirectly, through glyphs** (`../Glyphs/glyph-system.md`).
A glyph is a deity's power expressed at a safe remove — channeled through a mortal caster,
never the god in person. This is the deities' **only sanctioned way to act**, and it's why
the whole glyph grammar exists.

- **All glyph magic is deity-lent power.** The entire 5×5 grammar is the forgotten gods'
  indirect hand in the world.
- **But humans do not know this.** Survivors believe glyphs are a **decoded ancient
  technique** — craft recovered from old script, nothing more. The truth (that every cast
  is borrowing a forgotten, living god's power — that they are, unknowingly, praying) is a
  **lore reveal**, surfaced by the sword and the underground sanctum
  (`../Regions/region-set.md`).
- The **Light kill line** is the most overt end of this — the deities' anti-hive power
  lent most directly (`../Glyphs/glyph-system.md`).

## 4. The soul dimension & the 1v1 duel

Because occupation is **soul-on-soul**, the contest for a soul happens in a **soul
dimension** — the second-layer 1v1 (`../Sword/soul-duels.md`):

- To **sever** an Active or Named Node, you enter the soul dimension and fight for it
  there. You play **as the sword-girl** — soul against soul.
- The hive can **contest you** in that space. This is the same mechanism by which it could
  take a deity or a sword: the duel **is** the universal act of fighting over occupation.
- **Fail the duel and you die.** Real stakes — losing the soul contest is losing yourself.

So the soul duel is not a combat minigame bolted on; it is the **literal expression of the
whole metaphysics** — the one place where the possession economy is fought hand-to-hand.

## 5. Why this makes the swords what they are

- The **true sword (the maiden)** is a soul of the willing kind — close kin to the deities'
  nature, which is why she can do the full divine repertoire (fly, speak aloud, sever).
- The **rusty sword (yours)** is the **same kind of thing — a bound soul — but coerced and
  lesser**: a soul forced into a blade by a botched rite, rusted because it is illegitimate,
  burning with fire because it never consented (`premise-and-the-one-accord.md`). She is,
  ontologically, a small wounded cousin of the gods.
- The **One Accord** choice (`premise-and-the-one-accord.md`) is therefore a choice between
  two souls of the same lineage — and a forsaken one, being a soul with its own will, can
  go pursue its own path, even be contested or recruited by others.
