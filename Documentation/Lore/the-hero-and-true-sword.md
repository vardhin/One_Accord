# The Hero & The True Soul Sword

> Backstory that predates the player. Important for distinguishing the player's
> rusty sword from the legitimate original.

## The hero

A hero emerged during the plague crisis. He:

- Found the Rosetta-like tome.
- Learned spells from it, including **flame spells**.
- Taught a few followers.
- Sought the **true Soul Sword**.

## The sanctum on the divine mountain

The hero climbed a divine mountain and found a sanctum guarded by an **old man**.

The old man's condition: **someone must sacrifice themselves willingly** for the
sword to be lifted.

- The hero retreated.
- A **follower sacrificed themselves willingly** and became the true Soul Sword.
- The old man was released — and was apparently the **previous sword**.

This establishes the rite's true nature: **the true Soul Sword comes from
willingness / consent.** (This is the moral contrast with the rusty sword — see
`sword-girl-backstory.md`.)

## What the true Soul Sword could do

- Speak.
- Fly.
- Cast / channel spells; act as a catalyst.
- **Sever divinity.**

## The hero's partial victory and fall

- The hero made progress and placed some kind of **seal** that made the hivemind
  *stoppable / constrained* rather than absolutely omnipotent.
- By then the world was mostly gone.
- The hero eventually reached the hivemind core and **was defeated**.
- The **true Soul Sword vanished.**

## Consequence for the present

The seal is why, in the present, the hive is **finite and constrained** rather
than omnipotent — which is what makes the
[hive concentration mechanic](../Mechanics/hive-concentration.md) possible.

The vanished true sword is also why the **rusty imitation** the player finds
exists at all (see `sword-girl-backstory.md`).
