# Core Named NPC Roster (Brainstorm)

> Working list toward the ~40 named-NPC budget. Most of these are placeholders /
> role-slots, not finished characters. Heavily incomplete.

## Budget reminder

- **40 named NPCs is a HARD CAP** (concluded). Everything beyond is **ambient**
  (Tier 3 — unnamed/lightly named, not deeply simulated). Plan all content under
  this ceiling.
- Tier 0: 2 (Sword, Hivemind).
- Tier 1: ~10–12 (below).
- Tier 2: fills in under the cap as regions are authored — **not a list to invent
  abstractly now** (production content, bounded by the 40 cap).
- Tier 3: ambient, unnamed/lightly named (on top of / outside the 40 named).

(See `npc-tiers.md`.)

## The comparison standard — RESOLVED: no former-wielder NPC

Lore fixes this: the **cleric was the only person who ever wielded her**, and she
**killed him** by refusing to comply (`../Lore/sword-girl-backstory.md`). There is
no admired predecessor to compare the player to, so the old "former-wielder
candidate" role-slot is **deleted** — it contradicted canon.

The sword's "you fall short" standard is instead **aspirational, not historical**:
she measures the player against the **wielder she deserved but never had** — an
*imagined ideal* partner, not a real past holder. Bittersweet, canon-true, and
needs no NPC slot. See `../Sword/sword-as-npc.md` → "The imagined ideal wielder."

## Resolved / deferred

- **Names: deferred to per-NPC authoring.** Don't name empty role-slots; each NPC
  gets its Chinese name (fitting the world's language) when it becomes a real
  character. Not an open conceptual gap.
- **Tier 2: not invented abstractly.** Fills in under the **40 hard cap** as
  regions are authored (production content).
- **Infectable NPCs / Named Nodes: resolved — none.** The living cannot be
  infected (`../Mechanics/plague-and-infection.md`). Named Nodes are hive-grown
  merged threads, not people. The only human channel is the **soft-infected**
  (temporary blackout takeover).
