# Soul Duels — The Soul Dimension

> Where the player BECOMES the sword. The signature second-layer combat.

## The mechanic

Some enemies — especially active husks / nodes — **cannot be truly killed just by
beating their bodies.** After the player defeats the enemy's body, the sword
**enters or exposes a soul/void dimension.**

In that space:

- The player **controls the girl inside the sword** in a **1v1 sword fight.**
- The player's sprite **becomes the sword-girl.**

> Outside world: the protagonist uses the sword.
> Soul dimension: the player becomes the sword.

## Important correction

This is **NOT** an LLM debate. It is **not** the sword advocating in text. It is a
**literal 1v1 action fight.**

## Outcomes

- **If the sword-girl wins:** the enemy is **truly severed** from the
  hive/plague.
- **If she dies:** the player dies, or the bond is damaged.

## Gating

This happens **after soul bond** (i.e., it's not available from the start; it
unlocks once the relationship reaches the required depth).

## What affects her performance in the duel

The sword's **upgrades, relationship state, wounds, confidence, corruption,
resentment, and identity choices** affect her **performance / moveset / form** in
soul duels.

This means the player's choices everywhere else — how they upgraded her, whether
they respected consent, the depth of the bond — **literally reshape the
playable character** in these fights. (See `upgrades-and-identity.md` and
`../Mechanics/relationship-stats.md`.)

## Thematic note

Soul duels are **battles for severance and selfhood** — the clearest expression
of the possession-vs-partnership axis (`../genre-and-themes.md`). The hive
possesses; the duel severs.
