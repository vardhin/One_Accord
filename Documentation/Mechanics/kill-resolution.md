# Kill Resolution — Mutation, Fire, Silver, and Severance

> How a husk-kill actually resolves. This is the rule that turns the
> [hive concentration](hive-concentration.md) dial into a live economy: most kills
> are loans, not deaths. Touches combat, the bestiary, and every corpse custom in
> the world.

## The core rule

What a kill resolves into depends on **the tool** AND **how concentrated the body
is** (dumb husk vs. [Active / Named Node](../Enemies/hive-enemy-design.md)):

| Tool | Dumb husk | Active / Named Node | Character |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Plain metal** (any non-silver, non-soul weapon) | Loan — re-bodies in days, **mutated** | Loan — re-bodies, mutated | cheap, traversal; *always* a loan |
| **Fire** | **Permanent** true death — vessel destroyed | **Backfires** — knocked down, **respawns stronger** | cheap, loud, indiscriminate, needs fuel/pyrecraft |
| **Silver** | **Permanent** true death — clean & quiet | **Backfires** — knocked down, **respawns stronger** | premium, **scarce/hoarded**; no noise, no fuel, no drift spike |
| **Soul sword** (the girl) | Clean, cheap **severance** | **Soul duel** — the only true death | the *only* thing that kills the awake |

Two human tools — **fire and silver** — perma-kill **dumb husks**. Silver is
"fire you carry": same role, but clean, quiet, and rare instead of cheap and loud
(it predates fire as the first anti-hive defense — see
[ancient history](../Lore/ancient-history.md), and the scarcity is what caused the
**Silver Anarchy**). Neither works on the awake.

The **soul sword** is the player's rusty girl-sword — the *only* weapon capable of
**severance**, and the *only* thing that can truly kill an **Active / Named Node.**

Three hard facts fall out of this:

- **Fire and silver kill the dumb; only severance kills the awake.** Against an
  **Active**, both fire and silver are a *mistake* — the body dies briefly, then
  re-bodies **denser/stronger**, feeding the
  [concentration ratchet](hive-concentration.md).
- Because concentration *creates* Actives, **all your convenient tools degrade as
  you fight a region.** Late/hot regions backfire on fire and silver alike and
  funnel you toward the sword and soul duels — the built-in path to the "severance
  is the only verb left" endgame.
- **Plain metal never finishes anything** — it only ever loans, against dumb and
  awake alike.

> Misjudging a held body as dumb and torching (or silvering) it doesn't just fail
> — it makes the enemy stronger. Reading dumb-vs-awake is a real combat stake.

## Mutation — the body comes back shaped by how it died

A **plain-metal** kill does not stay dead (and neither does *any* kill on an
Active). The will re-bodies after a few days as **grotesque body-horror whose form
is signed by the method of the kill:**

- **Burned-but-not-enough** → charred, fused, smoke-leaking forms.
- **Hacked / dismembered** → things that reassemble wrong: extra-jointed, crawling
  on severed parts.
- **Crushed** → low, broken-backed, flattened horrors.
- **Bled / pierced** → drained, needle-thin, leaking.

Consequence: **a region's bestiary is partly authored by the player's own
violence.** The grotesques in a place are shaped like how the last people fought
through it. This also retro-explains the early-plague body-horror constructs in
[ancient history](../Lore/ancient-history.md) — they are centuries of accumulated
bad kills.

## Plain metal helps a little (but you'll regret it)

Plain-metal kills are not pure loans — **some attrition sticks**, so metal slowly
draws a region down, but inefficiently. Every swing also:

- Adds [concentration drift](hive-concentration.md) (sticky — the region stays
  hotter on return).
- Seeds a future mutant unless the body is dealt with (burned or silvered).

So plain metal is **"progress you'll regret":** viable, tempting, makes the place
worse on a delay. The disciplined player rations it and uses it mainly to **get
past husks now**, not to clear a region — that's what fire, silver, and the sword
are for.

## Fire — permanent on the dumb, a backfire on the awake

Fire destroys a **dumb husk's** vessel for good — there is nothing left to
re-body. But against an **Active / Named Node**, the will is concentrated enough
that burning the body only knocks it down; it **re-bodies stronger.** The visible
form of this failure is the **Pyre Husk** (see
[Arghanzza bestiary](../Enemies/Arghanzza-bestiary.md)): a held body the fire
woke rather than ended.

**Pyrecraft** (gated behind Arghanzza) is the skill of making sure a body is truly
**dumb / unheld before you burn it** — so it burns clean instead of waking. It does
not make fire work on Actives; nothing makes fire work on Actives. Only the soul
sword does.

> Burning corpses in this world is not hygiene. It is **denying the will a
> vessel** — but only a vessel it has already let go of. Every culture's
> pyre/ash/salt custom is mechanically load-bearing.

## Severance is tiered

- **Dumb husks** → a normal soul-sword strike severs them **cleanly and cheaply** —
  no duel, no mutation, no drift. The sword is the *clean* tool.
- **Actives & Named Nodes** → still require the full [soul duel](../Sword/soul-duels.md)
  where the player becomes the sword-girl. Keeps the duel special.

So the sword is surgical (no mutation tax, works on the awake) but limited by reach
and by whom she will fight; **fire** and **silver** are the permanent *dumb-clear*
tools — fire loud and cheap, silver quiet and scarce.

## The four-tool economy

Every region becomes a question of which tool you can afford on which body:

- **Plain metal** — get past husks now; always a loan, drives drift, seeds mutants.
- **Fire** — perma-clear dumb husks, but loud, fuel/pyrecraft-hungry, and
  **backfires on Actives.**
- **Silver** — perma-clear dumb husks **quietly and cleanly**, but silver is
  rare/hoarded so you ration it; also **backfires on Actives.**
- **Soul sword** — the only true death for the awake; clean cheap severance on the
  dumb.

This is the moment-to-moment expression of "you cannot go on a killing spree
without consequence" — plain metal loans; fire and silver pay against the dumb but
**punish you against the awake**; only the sword pays against everything.

> Whoever controls **silver** controls *quiet* survival — the one way to clear
> dumb husks without summoning attention. This is why a silver-trade house (the
> sword-girl's father's) mattered, and why a silver-rich place becomes a trade
> hub. See the region notes.
