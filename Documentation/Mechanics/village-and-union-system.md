# Village & Union System

> Progress is regional organization, not just personal leveling. The union is
> infrastructure, not an abstract reputation bar.

## Core loop

The player must convince villages, form unions, set up defenses, reduce husks,
organize teams, and coordinate survival.

> Progress is not just personal leveling. It is **regional organization.**

## Survivors are competent and suspicious

The player is **not** teaching basic survival. They are:

- Helping unite **hardened** communities.
- Solving **logistical conflicts.**
- Introducing **new capabilities** made possible by the sword.

(See `../Lore/setting-and-tech-level.md` — the player is a carrier between
specialized islands of competence.)

## The union is infrastructure

Villages may agree politically but still need roads cleared, bells restored,
quarantine protocols standardized, grain secured, scouts trained, defenses built.

### Possible village/union actions

Raise watchtowers · train militia · establish bell codes · build quarantine
houses · organize night patrols · appoint corpse-burners · create food reserves ·
send scouts · seal wells · set refugee routes · assign strike teams · detect
actives · build barricades · negotiate inter-village pacts · restore roads ·
repair bridges · protect food routes.

## Conflict, not consensus

Village conflicts give the system drama:

- A village **denies** the plague is coordinated because admitting it would
  collapse trade.
- A food region must decide **who eats.**
- A communication route must be **restored or sealed.**
- A forge must choose between **repairing defenses and upgrading the sword.**
- A quarantine breach threatens **trust.**
- A mother **hides an incubating child.**
- A leader **hides symptoms** to maintain morale.
- A village **refuses to join** because the player carries a talking/cursed sword.
- A community **worships or negotiates with the hive.**
- A village **scapegoats** sword-carriers, corpse-burners, refugees, or immune
  families.

## Ties into other systems

- **NPCs** staff and unlock all of this — every facility needs a person (see
  `../Npcs/core-named-roster.md` and `system-npc-roles.md`).
- **The Hivemind** strategically targets union cohesion, defenses, and knowledge
  spread (see `../Npcs/hivemind-agent.md`).
- An evolving **union camp / redoubt** (concept, not a designed region yet) could
  be the physical embodiment of accumulated union choices.
- Endgame win condition routes through union + protocols + severance (see
  `plague-and-infection.md`).
