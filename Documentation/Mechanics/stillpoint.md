# Stillpoint — The Combat Negotiation Window

> A bullet-time contest when player intent and sword will collide. Name candidate
> "Held Cut" also considered; **Stillpoint is the stronger name.**

## What it is

When the sword **objects** to an action, it can trigger a **bullet-time
contest** — not normal dialogue, a **fast combat negotiation window.**

- Time slows.
- The sword speaks.
- The player gets a few short arguments or responses.

## When it fires

Used **sparingly**, mainly for:

- Contested violence.
- Major moral choices.
- Fresh infected.
- Surrendering enemies.
- Human targets.
- Former allies.
- Ritual objects.
- Boss phase moments.

> Stillpoint happens when player intent and sword will collide.

## Outcomes

Allow lethal, allow nonlethal, defense only, delay, refuse, punish/backlash, or
**emergency override.**

## Arguments must be mechanically grounded

Arguments are **not free text** — the system checks real state.

Example: player argues *"he's infected."* The system checks:

- Does the player have **evidence**?
- Were **symptoms observed**?
- Does the sword **sense a hive thread**?
- Has the player **abused that excuse before**?
- Is the target **actually** infected?
- How much does the sword **trust** the player?

### Argument categories

necessity, mercy, evidence, restraint, "trust me," "obey me," remember,
vengeance, law, doubt/listen.

### Arguments are unlocked by play

The player should **not** always get every option. Options unlock through actual
play: evidence gathered, literacy, sword trust, region knowledge, observation,
previous choices, training, or corruption.

## Reverse Stillpoint

The sword can **initiate** Stillpoint when **it** wants to act and the player
hesitates. It can demand violence, mercy, retreat, severance, or refusal. (This
keeps the sword from being only a morality brake — see
`../Sword/combat-agency.md`.)

## Forcing through Stillpoint

The player can try to **dominate** the sword:

- **Success:** attack proceeds, **major relationship damage.**
- **Failure:** sword locks, drops, stamina shock, or backlash.
