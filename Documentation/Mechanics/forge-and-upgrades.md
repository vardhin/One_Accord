# Forge & Upgrade Infrastructure

> Upgrades come from rebuilding WORLD CAPABILITY, not a flat weapon tree. The
> identity/consent side of sword upgrades lives in
> `../Sword/upgrades-and-identity.md`; this file is the world systems that enable
> them.

## Principle

The sword gets upgraded because **the world regains the ability to modify,
understand, and train around weapons.** Upgrade progression requires
**infrastructure, expertise, and knowledge.**

### Infrastructure examples

Grindstone · anvil · bellows · furnace · forge parts · quenching trough · fuel
supply · clean water · workshop roof · safe storage · glyph-safe clamps ·
training yard.

### Expertise examples

Blacksmith · glyph master · trainer · rite/spell instructor · decoder/scribe ·
healer/plague doctor · quartermaster · guard captain · glassworker · furnace
master.

### Knowledge examples

Blueprints · glyph codes · forge diagrams · old manuals · oral traditions ·
failed experiments · enemy drops · recovered inscriptions · coded patterns.

## The upgrade loop

```text
Explore dangerous area
  → recover people / tools / knowledge / materials
  → improve settlement capability
  → NPCs research / craft / train
  → player & companions gain new options
  → sword reacts as an NPC
```

Basic improvement starts with finding a **grindstone**, then forge parts, then
blueprints, then decoding blueprints, then improving/rescuing the blacksmith.
This links upgrades to **exploration and NPC systems.**

## Forge levels

| Level | Name | Capability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Cold bench | Inspect, clean, wrap, sharpen badly |
| 1 | Working hearth | (needs grindstone, bellows, fuel, anvil) Repair edge, fit guard, rewrap hilt, basic tools |
| 2 | True heat | Temper, reinforce, repair cracks, socket simple housings |
| 3 | Pattern forge | Interpret blueprints, make specialized weapon bodies, **companion magic weapons**, glyph-embedding |
| 4 | Living / soul forge | Safely modify the **sentient sword** — requires consent, rare tools, maybe glyph master/healer support |

## Forge levels map to geography

The forge chain is **gated by place**, not just by improving one bench
(`../Regions/`):

- **Magizhee (L0→1)** — the starter limit: cold bench → working hearth (the
  grindstone → sharpening chain only). (`../Regions/Magizhee/region-magizhee.md`.)
- **Vendur (L1→2)** — the **basics**: a working forge for repair, sharpening, plain
  blades, tempering, simple housings. Vendur is "the silver place," not the master
  smith. (`../Regions/Vendur/vendur-detail.md`.)
- **Teling (L3→4)** — the **mastery gate**: glyph-embedding, silver wards, masterwork
  weapon bodies, companion magic weapons, and the soul-forge work on the sentient
  sword. Teling is the hereditary smith-guild / glyph-keeper region with old-hero
  links (the *making* counterpart to Vengarz's *doctrine*); the player must reach it
  to take the sword past L2. (`../Regions/region-set.md`.)

So **mastery is a destination**, reachable on the human-pocket side (Teling is SE of
Vendur), not a thing the first towns can do.

## Blacksmith progression IS character progression

The blacksmith improves because the **player improves their life and working
conditions:** food, safety, roof, tools, apprentice, old hammer, charcoal supply,
workshop defense, blueprints, materials.

## Other weapons

The player can pick other weapons later. They are **viable but not equivalent**:

- No sword consent, no Stillpoint, no soul-side combat, no deep NPC agency, no
  sword emergency saves.
- **Reliable but spiritually shallow.**
- Using them affects the sword relationship (see `../Sword/combat-agency.md`).

## Companion weapons (via glyphs)

The glyph master can eventually make magic weapons for teammates — **not as
special as the sword.** Examples: guard salt-pikes, scout bell-knife, healer
cold-needle, corpse-burner ash-axe. (See `../Glyphs/glyph-system.md`.)

## First settlement limit

The first settlement should **NOT** unlock full upgrades — only **basic
maintenance**, especially the **grindstone → sharpening** chain. (See
`../Regions/Magizhee/region-magizhee.md`.)

## Every activity needs an area AND an NPC

With ~7 regions and ~40 NPCs, every mechanic must have a home, a person, and a
reason to exist. (See `system-npc-roles.md`.)
