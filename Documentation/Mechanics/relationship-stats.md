# Relationship & Combat-State Stats

> These are relationship/combat-state progression — SEPARATE from material sword
> upgrades (`forge-and-upgrades.md`). Some overlap with the guide metrics in
> `../Sword/metrics-clarity-trust-resonance.md`.

## The stats

### Trust (long-term reliability)

Reliability between player and sword.

- **Rises:** respecting consent, keeping vows, telling truth, asking before
  modifications, careful violence, protecting the sword from exploitation.
- **Falls:** forcing, lying, violating vows, using the sword as a tool, bypassing
  its refusal cruelly.

### Attunement (metaphysical/combat compatibility)

> Renamed from "Resonance" to avoid collision with the sword's dialogue
> output-budget metric (`../Sword/metrics-clarity-trust-resonance.md`). These are
> now two distinct stats: **Attunement** = combat compatibility; **Resonance** =
> how much she can say.

- **Rises:** clean combat rhythm, good parries, soul duels, accepted glyph use,
  matching the sword's preferred fighting style.
- **Falls:** chaotic spam, incompatible glyphs, hive interference, overuse,
  fighting while the sword is fatigued.

### Sync (short-term)

Short-term combat rhythm **inside** an encounter.

### Autonomy

The sword's ability to **resist, initiate, or act independently.**

### Resentment

Tracks **negative memory / pressure** from violations.

### Fatigue

Tracks **overuse** of sword interventions or glyph channeling.

## Identity-axis stats (from the upgrade lens)

When viewed through upgrades/identity, the relevant axes are: **trust,
resentment, shame, hunger, autonomy, dependence, fear, bond depth, corruption,
resonance.** (See `../Sword/upgrades-and-identity.md`.)

## What these feed

- **Combat cooperation roll** and hit quality (`combat-system.md`).
- **Stillpoint** argument availability and outcomes (`stillpoint.md`).
- **Soul duel** performance/moveset/form (`../Sword/soul-duels.md`).
- **Dialogue** generosity and access (`../Sword/dialogue-system.md`).

## Naming note

The combat-compatibility stat is now **Attunement** (was "Resonance") so it no
longer collides with the dialogue **Resonance** (output budget). There is still
conceptual overlap between these combat-state stats and the Clarity/Trust/
Resonance guide metrics; they are documented separately for now, and whether to
unify them into one model is **not yet decided.**
